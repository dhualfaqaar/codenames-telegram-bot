Stockfish.js 10.0.2 WebAssembly worker bundle

Engine: Stockfish, compiled to WebAssembly by Niklas Fiekas / niklasf.
Package source: https://github.com/niklasf/stockfish.js
NPM package: https://registry.npmjs.org/stockfish.js/-/stockfish.js-10.0.2.tgz
Engine files are licensed under GNU GPL v3; see COPYING.txt.

This is the single-threaded WebAssembly engine. The .js worker and .wasm file
must remain together under /stockfish/ so the worker can load its sibling WASM.
